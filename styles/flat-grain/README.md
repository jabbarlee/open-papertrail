# Flat Grain

Relatable explainers: a question you've privately asked yourself, a plain answer you recognise at once, and a last line
that turns it back on you. Flat inks, hard-edged light, big black masses, film grain on every colour. 16-35 s.

- **Example:** `examples/walk/` ("Why do you only call your mom when you're walking somewhere?") → `../../examples/flat-grain/walk.mp4`
- **Rules:** `FORMAT.md` (idea, hook types, five-sentence script, picture), `DESIGN.md` (look), `VOICE.md` (`fern`, one uncut take), `MAKE.md` (build recipe)
- **Components:** `ink.ts` (grain tiles, curves, pens, stipple), `people.ts` (the bust), `stage.ts` (captions, bubbles, tags, wipes), `street.ts` (inks, a night street), `timeline.ts` (shots by word index)
