# Blend: two old roommates who stopped speaking still share a Spotify Blend, and it keeps talking for them.
My old roommate and I haven't spoken in two years, but we share a playlist.
It's a Spotify Blend, so every morning it mixes her songs with mine.
Last month her half was all sad songs at three in the morning.
I couldn't text her, so I played our road trip song forty times.
Next morning, it was the first song in the Blend. {pause=0.4}
She'd played it forty-one times. {pause=0.6}
We still haven't said a word. Our match is ninety-eight percent.
