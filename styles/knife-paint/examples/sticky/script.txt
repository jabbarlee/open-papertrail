# The Weak Glue: a chemist tries to make a super-strong glue, makes the weakest one, and it ends up on your fridge.
In nineteen sixty-eight, a 3M chemist tried to make an extremely strong glue, and failed. {pause=0.3}
Spencer Silver had made the opposite. {pause=0.3}
A glue so weak, you could peel it off and stick it back again. {pause=0.35}
Nobody wanted it. {pause=0.25}
For five years, he showed it to anyone who'd listen. {pause=0.4}
Then a colleague, Art Fry, had a problem in church. {pause=0.3}
His paper bookmarks kept falling out of his hymn book. {pause=0.35}
So he coated them in Silver's weak glue. {pause=0.25}
They stayed, and they peeled off clean. {pause=0.5}
But the first time 3M sold them, in four cities, they flopped. {pause=0.3}
They were called Press 'n Peel. {say="They were called Press and Peel." pause=0.45}
In nineteen eighty, they came back as Post-it Notes. {pause=0.5}
He was trying to make glue that never lets go. {pause=0.3}
It's on your fridge because it always does.
