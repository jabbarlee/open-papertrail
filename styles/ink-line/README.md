# Ink Line

First-person stories with no characters on screen: a phone, a note, a calendar, a playlist carries the story. One
brush-pen line on bone paper; cobalt only for what's going on inside the narrator. Word-by-word captions. 16-30 s.

- **Example:** `examples/blend/` (two old roommates who stopped talking still share a playlist) → `../../examples/ink-line/blend.mp4`
- **Rules:** `FORMAT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `VOICE.md` (`jules`), `MAKE.md`
- **Components:** `episode.ts` (the shell: `inkEpisode`, `wordT`, `spoken`), `ink.ts` (the brush line), `screen.ts` (phone, bubbles, rules, waveforms), `type.ts` (write-on text), `caption.ts` (word-by-word captions), `paper.ts`, `palette.ts`, `sound.ts`
