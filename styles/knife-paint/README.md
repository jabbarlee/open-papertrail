# Knife Paint

Narrated true chains of events from history, science and culture, from a surprising checkable fact to something the
viewer owns today. An acrylic palette-knife painting on canvas, with articulated figures that walk and gesture. 20-55 s.
Every fact is checked and listed in `sources.json`.

- **Example:** `examples/sticky/` (the weak glue that became the Post-it Note) → `../../examples/knife-paint/sticky.mp4`
- **Rules:** `FORMAT.md` (incl. the `sources.json` shape), `DESIGN.md`, `VOICE.md` (`archer`, voiced in pieces), `MAKE.md`
- **Components:** `components/README.md` (paint engine, episode shell, 17 backdrops, figures, props)
