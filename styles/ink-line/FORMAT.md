# Format: Ink Line

Short first-person stories told by one voice and drawn by one brush-pen line. **No characters on screen, ever**: no
faces, no figures, no mascots. The story is carried by the things in it: a phone screen, a note, a calendar, a
voicemail waveform, a door, a number, a line that becomes a road. The narrator is never seen; the viewer becomes the
"I". 16-30 seconds. Look: `DESIGN.md`. Voice: `VOICE.md`. Worked example: `examples/`.

## The idea
- **One true-feeling story from an ordinary life** about the people in it: friends, family, the group chat,
  remembering someone. Told in the first person, like telling a friend.
- **It's a story, not an explainer.** Something happens, it escalates, it turns. No "studies show", no tips, no
  lesson. A fact may appear only if it's part of the story (then it needs a source in `sources.json`).
- **Built on one specific, drawable object** the viewer has in their own life and can picture instantly, working
  exactly as it really works (a scheduled email, a shared album, a read receipt). The object is the main character.
- **The clarity test:** a stranger understands the whole situation from the hook alone, with no setup.
- **The ending** is the most surprising line, with an edge, and you can't guess it from the hook. It reframes
  everything before it. Never sweet, never a moral, never a tag line.
- **Cobalt means "inside me"**: what the narrator thinks, wants, hides or realises. Everything else is ink.

## Dead zones
Grief-bait (a dead parent's last voicemail and similar tearjerkers), stock sketches (typing and deleting at 2 a.m.,
the wrong group chat), "moms be like", the parent-who-secretly-cares template, and any idea that needs a person's
face to land.

## Structure (16-30 s)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| 0-2 s | The hook, spoken, with its 2-5 word title written on from frame 0 and the object already being drawn |
| Middle | 5-7 beats, a new drawing every 2-3 s. Each beat changes scale or object; the line carries from one to the next |
| Payoff | One held beat of silence, then the last line; the drawing turns into something it couldn't have been before |
| Loop | The last frame leads back into the first |

## Script
55-80 words, 6-9 lines of full natural sentences in one voice. Line 1 is the hook, the last line is the payoff.
`{pause=0.6}` before the payoff only. Numbers as words.
